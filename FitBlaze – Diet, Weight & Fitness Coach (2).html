A plan for every body

Your goal decides your calories. Pick the path that fits you.

🔥 Lose weight

A moderate calorie deficit, high protein and daily movement. Aim for 0.25–0.75 kg (0.5–1.5 lb) per week – slow and steady keeps the muscle and the results.

💪 Gain healthy weight

For lean or underweight people: a small calorie surplus, lots of protein, strength training and regular meals to build muscle, not just fat.

⚖️ Stay balanced

Maintain your weight with good habits: balanced plates, hydration, sleep and regular exercise.

Nutrient Timing

Total calories matter most, but timing helps performance and recovery.

2–3 hours before training
Balanced meal: slow carbs (oats, rice, potato) + lean protein + little fat.
30–60 minutes before
Light carb snack (banana, dates, toast) if you feel hungry. Sip water.
During (over 60 min)
Water, plus electrolytes or a small carb source for long, intense sessions.
Within 2 hours after
20–40 g protein + carbs to refill energy (chicken and rice, eggs and toast, yogurt and fruit).
Evening
Protein-rich meal, plenty of vegetables. A casein-rich snack (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese) supports overnight recovery.
Every day
Spread protein over 3–5 meals (about 0.3 g per kg of body weight each) and sleep 7–9 hours.
